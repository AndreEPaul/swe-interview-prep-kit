# Recall sheet

A one-page review aid for the last day or two before an interview. Build it only from stories you have already confirmed, not new material. Two to four stories are enough.

- Interview and date:
- Question types I might get (conflict, failure, ambiguity, leading without authority, and so on):

## Story: [short name]

- One-line hook:
- My own decision or action:
- Result and its evidence (measured / estimated / planned / unknown):
- What I learned or what I changed:
- Likely follow-up to be ready for:

Copy this block for each story.

## Check before the interview

- Is each story a real event, with nothing added for effect?
- Does each story say what I did, not only what the team did?
- Can I explain where each number came from and what it was measured against?
- Can I tell each story in about two minutes, and shorter if asked?

This is a reminder, not a script to memorize. Do not fill gaps with invented details. Keep filled sheets outside this repository; save only when requested to a confirmed private path.
